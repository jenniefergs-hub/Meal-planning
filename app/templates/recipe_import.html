From a URL

Works best on recipe blogs and sites that embed structured recipe data (most modern recipe sites do). Not every page is supported — you'll always get a chance to review and fix everything before saving.

From a photo

Take a clear, well-lit, straight-on photo of a cookbook page. Text recognition is best-effort — ingredients and instructions are split by a rough guess, so review carefully before saving. Calories are almost never printed on a page, so you'll usually need to add that yourself.
